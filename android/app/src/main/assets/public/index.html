Mana Dukanam మన దుకాణం

Find the nearby shop that is open, has your items and gives a good price. Both phones share one live state: change a price on the shopkeeper phone and the customer phone re-ranks.

Try it in two minutes
  1. Customer phone: tap Compare shops for onion 1 kg and rice 5 kg.
  2. Shopkeeper phone, Rates tab: set rice to 55. The customer list re-ranks.
  3. Customer: tap Ask all open shops. The shopkeeper gets a request and sends a quote.
  4. Customer picks a quote and orders. Shopkeeper accepts, marks ready, enters the pickup code.
  5. Shopkeeper phone, Doubts tab: the 12 questions shopkeepers will ask you.

What this fixes

Gaps found in the apps already on the market, and what this prototype does about each. Vijayawada specifics are marked.

Stock is only a catalogueQuote mode: the shop confirms in one tap. Prices older than a day are flagged as "may be old".
Shopkeepers will not type 200 pricesA rate board of 12 staples with plus/minus buttons, voice update and a one-tap "add item".
Small shops cannot pay for marketingEach shop gets a link and QR to share with regulars. Neighbourhood search is free.
Orders get lost between appsOne shop, one order, one pickup code. No-shows can be released. UPI goes straight to the shop.
Credit customers are left outKhata with a limit per customer, WhatsApp reminders, and the customer sees their own balance.
English-first appsTelugu shop screens, Telugu item names, and search by Telugu words typed in English (ullipaya, biyyam).
Vegetable prices swing daily (Vijayawada)Rythu Bazaar reference rate beside vegetables, and a 30 minute price lock on every quote. The rate needs a data source or manual entry.
Small baskets are not worth deliveryPickup first, no minimum order, and the shop sets its own delivery fee and radius.
Local festivals (Vijayawada)Ready-made lists: monthly ration, daily vegetables, Diwali and Karthika Masam sweets kit.
Local areas (Vijayawada)Labbipet, Benz Circle, Governorpet, Patamata, Moghalrajpuram, Bhavanipuram, Gunadala, Auto Nagar, Kanuru and more.

Shop names, prices, ratings and counts are invented for this demo. Telugu text should be checked by a native speaker before shopkeepers see it.
